# App Store screenshots — drop folder

Put captures in the matching folder. I validate dimensions and content, then
upload them to App Store Connect.

    iphone-6.9/   from Aphelion (iPhone 16 Pro Max)
    ipad-13/      from Codex (M5 iPad Pro)
    mac/          from this Mac

Shoot **native** — do not resize or crop. I check the exact pixel sizes on
arrival and tell you if anything needs re-shooting. 2–10 per folder; order is
set at upload, so name them `01-…`, `02-…` in the order you want them shown.

## What Apple will reject

> "Screenshots must show the app in actual use. Not permitted: title art alone,
> **login screens**, splash screens, or screens from other platforms."

The join/enroll screen is a login screen for this purpose. Every shot needs the
console showing a live mesh.

## Careful: these become a permanent public page

The worldview holds real observations — roster entries, who is present and how,
sensor readings, theories about the people served. Screenshots of TheRiver
publish that to the App Store, and screenshots cannot be changed on an approved
version.

Prefer a mesh seeded with fictional observations, or stick to screens that carry
no personal detail. Before uploading I flag anything I can read in the frame:
human names, device labels, locations, heart rate, coordinates.

## Shot list (best first — screenshot 1 does most of the work)

1. **The sphere, populated** — the worldview globe with real structure visible.
   The signature image; it should be first.
2. **The roster** — nodes with presence and connectivity badges
   (⇄ tailscale / ⇢ lighthouse / · local). Shows it is a mesh, not an app.
3. **A theory or question** — the familiar having noticed something and asking,
   with the answer-in-place field. Shows it thinks rather than waits for prompts.
4. **The Device screen** — SERVING, oracle state, consent toggles. Shows
   on-device Apple Intelligence and that consent is explicit.
5. *(optional)* **Watch or gates** — the covenant boundary, or the watch on the
   roster.

Mac wants its own set from the desktop console at the same beats.
